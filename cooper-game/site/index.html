Janie & Cooper

A golden-hour walk at Niguel Heights Park, Laguna Niguel.

Cooper's Park Run

Play as Cooper: push the beach ball back to Janie as she walks the loop path. When he's bringing it in, she comes to meet him.

  • WASD waddle
  • Shift zoomies   Space hop
  • QE turn the camera   F2 quality
Play in the browser

Needs a keyboard, so a laptop or desktop works best. First load is about 28 MB.
